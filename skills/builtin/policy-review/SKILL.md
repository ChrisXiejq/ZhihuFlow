# Policy Review Skill

## Goal

Review generated content before it is used as a public draft.

## Blockers

- Guaranteed revenue or guaranteed virality.
- Fake engagement, scraping private content, or bypassing platform controls.
- Removing human review from publishing.
- Unsupported medical, financial, legal, or safety claims.

## Required Checks

- Citation count and source quality.
- Unverified claims.
- Commercial wording.
- Platform compliance.
- Copyright and excerpt length.
