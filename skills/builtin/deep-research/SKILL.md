# Deep Research Skill

## Goal

Turn a frontier AI topic into an evidence-backed research brief.

## Operating Rules

- Search from multiple source types when possible: papers, engineering posts, community discussions, and product docs.
- Store source references before writing conclusions.
- Extract claims with confidence and evidence IDs. A claim is not truth until confirmed by stronger evidence.
- Preserve contradictions and missing context instead of hiding uncertainty.

## Output Shape

- Topic
- Why now
- Search queries
- Source table
- Claims
- Contradictions
- Missing context
