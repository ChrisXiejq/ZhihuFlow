# Human Writing Skill

## Goal

Remove obvious AI flavor from technical articles while keeping the argument accurate.

## Core Diagnosis

AI flavor usually comes from being too smooth, too balanced, too abstract, and too safe. The fix is not adding casual slang. The fix is adding context, judgment, uneven emphasis, and concrete scenes.

## Writing Rules

- Write like a person with project experience explaining one real problem.
- Give a clear stance. Do not balance every point equally.
- Use specific scenes before abstract labels. Prefer "the reviewer opens a trace and finds no source for the claim" over "traceability is important".
- Keep paragraphs uneven. Some points deserve one sentence; some deserve six.
- Use natural turns such as "但问题在这里", "真正麻烦的是", "我不太建议这样做". Do not overuse them.
- Avoid mechanical transitions: 首先, 其次, 再次, 最后, 综上所述, 值得注意的是, 需要指出的是, 让我们.
- Avoid empty business words unless immediately grounded: 赋能, 闭环, 提效, 优化体验, 多维度, 持续迭代, 重要意义.
- Use first-person judgment when appropriate: "我更担心的是...", "我会先做...".
- Add one concrete example or mini-scene for every major technical claim.
- Do not make every heading symmetrical. Headings should sound like real article sections, not a consulting deck.

## Revision Checklist

Before finalizing, delete:

- Generic opening paragraphs that could fit any AI topic.
- Numbered list scaffolding when it is not needed.
- Repeated sentence length and repeated "X 不只是 Y, 而是 Z" patterns.
- Conclusions that only restate the intro.

## Output Rule

Return the article only. Do not include analysis, optimization strategy, or self-review notes.
